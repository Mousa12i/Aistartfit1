3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show advertising and we do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • We never use your health, fitness or nutrition data for advertising, marketing or data mining, and we do not share it with anyone for those purposes.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models, and our AI provider does not either (see section 5).

6. How long we keep data

  • We keep your data while you have an account, so the app can show your history and plans.
  • You can delete individual entries (meals, pain reports, memory entries, coach chats, cycle data) in the app at any time.
  • When you delete your account (section 7), your account and all its data are deleted from our live database straight away.
  • Copies in our hosting provider’s backups are removed when those backups expire, after [[Number of days your Supabase plan keeps backups]] days.
  • OpenAI keeps data sent to it for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring. Stripe keeps payment records as the law requires it to.

7. Deleting your account

In the app: Account → Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete account, then type DELETE. This works straight away and you do not need to contact us.

Without the app: email [[Privacy contact email address]] from the email address of your StartFit account with the subject “Delete my StartFit account”. We check that the request comes from the account holder, then delete the account within one month at the latest (usually within a few days) and confirm by email.

  • Deleted: your account, profile, workouts and plans you made, meals and nutrition data, check-ins, recovery, pain, return-to-training and cycle data, coach conversations and memory, consent records, and everything else listed in section 2 that is stored in your account.
  • A running subscription is cancelled first so you are not charged again, and we ask Stripe to delete your customer record. Stripe keeps payment records as the law requires it to.
  • Kept for a limited time: copies in our hosting provider’s backups until they expire ([[Number of days your Supabase plan keeps backups]] days), data already sent to OpenAI for up to 30 days, and our hosting provider’s technical logs (which can contain your account identifier) until they expire automatically.
  • Data kept only on your device (section 2) is removed when you delete your account in the app; otherwise remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing its website data.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access — ask us for a copy of your data.
  • Rectification — correct your data in the app or ask us.
  • Erasure — delete your account in the app or by email (section 7).
  • Restriction and objection — ask us to limit or stop some uses.
  • Portability — ask us for your data in a commonly used electronic format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy & Data: switch off the AI coaches, switch off cycle insights, or withdraw consent to health-data processing. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before. Because health data is at the heart of StartFit, the app cannot work without that consent; you can then delete your account.
  • To use any right, contact [[Privacy contact email address]]. We will reply within one month.
  • You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk). We would appreciate the chance to help first.

9. Children

StartFit is not for anyone under [[Minimum age, e.g. 16]]. We do not knowingly collect data from children under that age; if you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

  • Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Every table of personal data is protected by row-level security, so each account can only read and change its own data.
  • Server keys that can reach all data are kept only on our servers, never in the app.

11. Data from Apple Health or Health Connect

The current version of StartFit does not read data from Apple Health, Health Connect or wearables. If a future version offers this, it will only do so after you allow it on your device, we will list exactly which data types it reads, and we will update this policy before that version is released.

12. Not medical advice

StartFit gives general fitness, nutrition and recovery guidance and estimates. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you have symptoms such as chest pain, fainting, or dark urine after exercise, stop and seek medical help.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the version date below. If the change affects how we use your health data or who we share it with, we will ask for your consent again in the app before it applies to you.
