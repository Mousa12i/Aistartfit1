Delete your StartFit account

StartFit · Version 2026-10-14

How to delete your account and data

In the app: Account → Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete account, then type DELETE. This works straight away and you do not need to contact us.

Without the app: email [[Privacy contact email address]] from the email address of your StartFit account with the subject “Delete my StartFit account”. We check that the request comes from the account holder, then delete the account within one month at the latest (usually within a few days) and confirm by email.

  • Deleted: your account, profile, workouts and plans you made, meals and nutrition data, check-ins, recovery, pain, return-to-training and cycle data, coach conversations and memory, consent records, and everything else listed in section 2 that is stored in your account.
  • A running subscription is cancelled first so you are not charged again, and we ask Stripe to delete your customer record. Stripe keeps payment records as the law requires it to.
  • Kept for a limited time: copies in our hosting provider’s backups until they expire ([[Number of days your Supabase plan keeps backups]] days), data already sent to OpenAI for up to 30 days, and our hosting provider’s technical logs (which can contain your account identifier) until they expire automatically.
  • Data kept only on your device (section 2) is removed when you delete your account in the app; otherwise remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing its website data.

Full details: StartFit Privacy Policy.
